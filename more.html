Peg Dolls

🧍

Our handcrafted peg dolls are a celebration of simplicity and creativity. Created from wooden pegs and decorated with love, these charming figures capture the essence of traditional toy-making with a magical twist.

Each peg doll is unique, featuring hand-painted details and dressed in carefully selected vintage fabrics and embellishments. These beloved classic toys are perfect for imaginative play, collections, or as thoughtful gifts that celebrate the art of handmade magic.

Whether you're looking for a whimsical playmate or a nostalgic treasure, our peg dolls bring the warmth and wonder of childhood magic to any age.

Musical Box Miniatures

🎵

Our enchanting musical box miniatures combine the magic of movement and melody. These delicate, intricate creations feature handcrafted figures that dance and twirl as gentle music plays, creating an atmosphere of wonder and nostalgia.

Each musical box miniature is a work of art, combining careful craftsmanship with the mechanics of traditional music boxes. Perfect as collectibles, display pieces, or special gifts, these creations bring the timeless magic of classic music boxes into a miniature format.

Whether you collect music boxes, love miniatures, or simply appreciate beautiful handmade items, our musical box miniatures are sure to enchant and delight with their delicate magic and melodic charm.
