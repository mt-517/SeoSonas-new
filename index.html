Meet Helen

Hello! I'm Helen, the maker behind SeoSonas – which translates from Welsh as "this is happiness." Ever since I was little, I've been captivated by the folklore surrounding the realm of Magic and Faerie, and the characters that live in the corners of our imagination. As a child, I developed a love of creating fantasy figures from scraps of vintage materials, pipe cleaners, wooden clothes pegs, recycled wools and yarns I had collected from my mother and grandmother, to make into gifts for my family and friends.

After years spent on other paths, I've reignited that childhood passion. Now an advocate for all things Faerie, I am inspired to bring out the unique and hopefilled character of each figure you'll find here. Just like real magic, these figures carry a uniqueness and unpredictability that only comes from true hand‑crafting.

Thank you for sharing in my joy. SeoSonas creations truly mean happiness for me, and I hope a little of that happiness reaches you through these tiny folk.
